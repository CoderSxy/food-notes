# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Logged-in people recording and browsing food places and items on PC and mobile browsers.
## Product Purpose
Keep shared records of places and items, visualize located records and recommend nearby food from recorded data.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Chinese UI. Default personal scope, switchable shared scope. Place coordinates required; item coordinates optional. Shared read, owner-only delete. Username/password and test OTP login. Existing Next.js/React/Supabase/Leaflet stack.
## Brand Commitments
Name: 美食地图. Reference: workbuddy-manager's centered application content, desktop bottom Dock and mobile expanding menu. Approved design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-responsive-dashboard-redesign.md.
